U.S. stocks drop as Nvidia slides and the fog of Trump's trade war thickens - NPR's Steve Inskeep

Nvidia's stock rose 1.1 percent after the news, and it's expected to fall further as investors look for ways to maintain their bets on the company.

The stock is now down 2.7 percent, or 0.85 percent, against the S&P 500.

The Dow Jones Industrial Average (DJIA) is up 9.7 percent, or 0.76 percent, against the Nasdaq Composite, which was up 3 percent.

"The market is being driven out of control by Donald Trump's candidacy, and that's a big cause for concern for investors," said Jeffrey S. Hochstein, who owns the Nasdaq and Nasdaq Composite. "The market is going to be forced out of control by Trump's candidacy and for many of your investors."
