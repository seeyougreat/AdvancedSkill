The social media giant said on Tuesday it had used "ethical and legal standards" to protect its privacy and security.

"We have the absolute right to make decisions that are in our best interests," Mr Zuckerberg said in a statement.

"We also believe that the way we use, engage, and use technology is to be safe and secure.

"The only way in which we can ensure that we are not doing this is if we use technology that is not ethical.

"We have set a very clear example on this, but we are still very much taking our responsibility to protect our users and to respect all privacy."
