Defence secretary pledges £450m military 'surge' to Ukraine - BBC Newsnight

The US Secretary of State, John Kerry, said Europe was ready to take "whatever steps are necessary" to protect civilians from the ongoing conflict.

Speaking after a joint news conference with German Chancellor Angela Merkel, Mr Kerry said he was confident that the UK was prepared to stand up for its people in the fight against terrorism.

He said: "We have to stay strong, we have to stay strong, and we have to stay strong in the face of this menace."

Last week the UK and its allies were accused of blocking the flow of arms to the pro-Russian separatists in eastern Ukraine.

The UK, a Nato member, is also said to be keen to secure Nato members' "safe zones" in eastern Ukraine.

The White House said that US President Barack Obama and German Chancellor Angela Merkel had agreed yesterday to help Ukraine achieve its "right to defend itself".

Speaking to German Chancellor Angela Merkel, Mr Kerry said: "The world has to act now, not later."

He said EU leaders are united in their commitment to "protect our interests" and Ukraine is working to ensure it is safe for its citizens.
